Runsheet Privacy Policy

Effective July 1, 2026

Runsheet is a cable installation tracker for field crews, developed by Marshal Burton ("we", "us"). This policy describes what data the app handles and how. The short version: we collect only what the app needs to work, we don't run ads or analytics, we don't track you, and we never sell or share your data with third parties for their own purposes.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address and a display name, used to create and sign in to your account.
  • Project content you create: projects, cable identifiers, work-stage status updates (recorded with who made them and when), notes, locations, cable uses, blocker flags and notes, and blocker photos you choose to attach.

That's the full list. The app does not collect your device location, contacts, browsing data, advertising identifiers, or any analytics or usage telemetry.

How it's used

Solely to provide the service: syncing your crew's cable status data between the members of a project, attributing status changes to the teammate who made them, and generating the reports you export. Nothing else.

Who can see it

  • Your project teammates: project content is shared with the other members of any project you join — that's the point of the app.
  • Our infrastructure provider: data is stored with Supabase, which processes it on our behalf. We do not share your data with anyone else.
  • Blocker photos: photos are stored at long, unguessable web addresses. They are not listed or searchable anywhere, but anyone who has the exact address of a photo can view it. Don't photograph anything sensitive.

Where it lives

Your project data is stored on our servers (hosted by Supabase) and, for offline use in the field, in a local copy on your device. All transfers between the app and our servers are encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Deleting the app removes the local copy.

Deleting your data

  • Content: cables and projects can be deleted in the app by project admins.
  • Your account: you can permanently delete your account in the app (Projects screen → Delete account). This removes your sign-in and profile immediately and cannot be undone. Project content remains available to the other members of your projects; your name is no longer attached to past status updates.
  • You can also email us (below) to request deletion.

Children

Runsheet is a professional tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
