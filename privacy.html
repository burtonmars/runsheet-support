Runsheet Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

Runsheet is a cable installation tracker for field crews, developed by Marshal Burton ("we", "us"). This policy describes what data the app handles and how. The short version: we collect only what the app needs to work, we don't run ads or analytics, we don't track you, and we never sell or share your data with third parties for their own purposes.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address and a display name, used to create and sign in to your account and to show your teammates who did the work. You can change your display name at any time in the app (tap your initials at the top right of the Projects screen).
  • Project content you create: projects, cable identifiers, work-stage status updates (recorded with who made them and when), notes, locations, cable uses, constraint flags and notes (recorded with who raised them and when), constraint photos you choose to attach, material stock counts (recorded with who last changed each count and when), the project number, and deliveries received on site — when and by whom, the PO or packing-slip number, a note, and photos of the packing slip and any damage.
  • Change history: a record of each change made to a cable — what was changed, the value before and after, who made the change, the time it was made on the device and the time it reached our servers, and the technical identifier your device's app or browser sends with each request (for example, browser and operating system version).

That's the full list. The app does not collect your device location, contacts, browsing data, advertising identifiers, or any analytics or usage telemetry.

How it's used

Solely to provide the service: syncing your crew's cable status data between the members of a project, attributing status changes to the teammate who made them, generating the reports you export, and troubleshooting a cable's record when its status is in question. Nothing else.

Who can see it

  • Your project teammates: project content is shared with the other members of any project you join — that's the point of the app. This includes each cable's change history, which any member of the project can view.
  • Our infrastructure provider: data is stored with Supabase, which processes it on our behalf. We do not share your data with anyone else.
  • Photos: constraint, packing-slip and damage photos are stored privately. Only signed-in members of the project can view them; the app opens each photo through a link that expires within an hour.
  • Delivery confirmations: when you share a delivery (for example, to your warehouse), the app hands the photos and message to your phone's share sheet, and you send it from your own email app. Runsheet doesn't send email itself.

Where it lives

Your project data is stored on our servers (hosted by Supabase) and, for offline use in the field, in a local copy on your device. All transfers between the app and our servers are encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Deleting the app removes the local copy.

Deleting your data

  • Content: cables and projects can be deleted in the app by project admins. Deleting a cable or a project also deletes its change history. Change history is otherwise kept for as long as the project exists.
  • Your account: you can permanently delete your account in the app (tap your initials at the top right of the Projects screen → Delete account). This removes your sign-in and profile immediately and cannot be undone. Project content remains available to the other members of your projects. Your display name, as it was when you deleted your account, is retained on the work you already recorded — the status updates you marked and when, and your entries in cables' change history — as a record of who performed the work; no other personal information about you is kept.
  • You can also email us (below) to request deletion.

Children

Runsheet is a professional tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
