Runsheet Support

Cable installation tracking for field crews

Runsheet keeps a low-voltage crew's cable pull sheet in everyone's pocket: generate your cable IDs once, then mark pulls, terminations, and testing as they happen — even with no signal in the building. Progress rolls up live for the whole project, and exports produce the closeout spreadsheet.

Common questions

  • Joining a project: ask your project admin for the six-character join code, then use "Join with code" on the Projects screen.
  • Working offline: just keep tapping — updates are saved on your phone and sync automatically when you have service again.
  • Deleting your account: Projects screen → Delete account. This is permanent; project data remains with your team.
